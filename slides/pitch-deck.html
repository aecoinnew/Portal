Who we are

A private investment firm built for the GCC.

Established in 2018, Emcoin Investment serves a select base of private clients across the Gulf with multi-asset portfolio management. Our platform handles both market-priced instruments and manually-valued alternatives — sukuk, private equity, and fund mandates — under a single, auditable system.
